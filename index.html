Our story

A family name you can trust

GDC Fire & Security (NI) Ltd is a proud, multi-award-winning, family-run fire and security company based in Belfast. With over 30 years of combined experience, we protect homes and businesses right across Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland and mainland UK.

We carry out the supply, installation, commission, maintenance and repair of CCTV, intruder alarm, access control and fire alarm systems — accredited by the SSAIB and BAFE so every job is done to recognised industry standards.

Being family-run means it's personal. We treat your property like our own, explain things in plain English, and we're here whenever you need us — day or night.

FAQs

Questions, answered

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Belfast and cover all of Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland and mainland UK for both domestic and commercial customers.

Are you accredited?

Yes. GDC is accredited by the SSAIB for intruder alarms, CCTV and access control, and by BAFE for fire alarm systems — so every install meets recognised industry standards.

Do you offer 24/7 monitoring?

We do. We provide alarm and fire system monitoring and maintenance with support available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Do you provide free estimates?

Always. We carry out a free, no-obligation survey of your property and provide a clear written estimate before any work begins.

Do you work with homes and businesses?

Both. From a single home alarm to a full commercial fire, CCTV and access control system, our team handles the supply, installation, commission, maintenance and repair.
