Fire & security specialists

Protecting homes & businesses across Northern Ireland

At GDC Fire & Security Ltd, we provide professional fire and security solutions for homes and businesses across Belfast and Northern Ireland. As a multi-award-winning, family-run company, we specialise in the design, installation and maintenance of fire alarm systems, intruder alarms, CCTV, access control, emergency lighting and fire extinguishers — delivering reliable, fully compliant solutions tailored to every customer.

From free site surveys and expert advice to installation, ongoing maintenance and 24/7 emergency support, our experienced engineers provide quality workmanship, exceptional customer service and a single trusted point of contact to keep your property protected, your systems compliant and your business operating with confidence.
