Our story

Built on trust, backed by accreditation

At GDC Fire & Security (NI) Ltd, we're a multi-award-winning, family-run business dedicated to protecting people, property and businesses across Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland and mainland UK.

We specialise in the design, installation, commissioning, maintenance and repair of fire and security systems, including CCTV, intruder alarms, access control and fire detection. As an SSAIB, BAFE + PSA accredited company, we deliver solutions that are fully compliant, dependable and tailored to the unique needs of every client.

What sets us apart is our personal approach. We believe in building long-term relationships through honest advice, meticulous attention to detail and exceptional customer service. Whether it's a single-site installation or a nationwide maintenance contract, our experienced team is committed to delivering the highest standards of workmanship and ensuring every project is completed professionally, efficiently and with care.

Accreditations

Independently inspected & approved

Accreditation isn't a logo on a van — it means our work is independently audited against recognised industry standards.

Intruder alarms · CCTV · Access control · NIRE140

The SSAIB is a leading certification body for security service providers in the UK and Ireland. Our SSAIB registration means our intruder alarm, CCTV and access control installations are independently inspected against recognised security standards.

Fire alarm systems · Registered ID 302579

BAFE is the independent register of quality fire safety service providers. Our BAFE registration covers the design, installation, commissioning and maintenance of fire detection and alarm systems — so your premises stay safe and compliant.
