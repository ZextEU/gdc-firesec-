Our story

Built on trust, backed by accreditation

GDC Fire & Security (NI) Ltd is a proud, multi-award-winning, family-run fire and security company based in Belfast. We protect homes and businesses right across Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland and mainland UK.

We handle the supply, installation, commission, maintenance and repair of CCTV, intruder alarm, access control and fire alarm systems — accredited by the SSAIB and BAFE so every job meets recognised industry standards.

Because we're family-run, the person who surveys your property, the engineer who installs your system and the voice on the end of the phone all care about the same thing: doing the job properly, first time.

Accreditations

Independently inspected & approved

Accreditation isn't a logo on a van — it means our work is independently audited against recognised industry standards.

SSAIB

Intruder alarms · CCTV · Access control

The SSAIB is a leading certification body for security service providers in the UK and Ireland. Our SSAIB registration means our intruder alarm, CCTV and access control installations are independently inspected against recognised security standards.

BAFE

Fire alarm systems · Registered ID 302579

BAFE is the independent register of quality fire safety service providers. Our BAFE registration covers the design, installation, commissioning and maintenance of fire detection and alarm systems — so your premises stay safe and compliant.
