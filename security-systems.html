Intruder alarm monitoring

Intruder alarm monitoring ensures every activation is responded to promptly, whether your premises are occupied or not. Connecting your system to a professionally monitored Alarm Receiving Centre (ARC) enables a rapid response and minimises the impact of unauthorised access.

Protecting a single site or multiple locations, our monitored solutions deliver continuous protection 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
