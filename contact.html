FAQ

Common questions

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Belfast and cover all of Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland and mainland UK for both domestic and commercial customers.

Are you accredited?

Yes. GDC is accredited by the SSAIB for intruder alarms, CCTV and access control, and by BAFE for fire alarm systems.

Do you offer 24/7 monitoring?

Yes — we provide alarm and fire system monitoring and maintenance with support available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

How much does a system cost?

Every property is different, so we start with a free, no-obligation survey and provide a clear written quote — no pressure and no hidden costs.

Do you work with both homes and businesses?

Yes — we design, install and maintain systems for domestic and commercial properties, from a single home alarm to a complete commercial fire and security package.
