Client brief

The brief

Tobar Mhuire is a much-loved period building and community retreat centre. Protecting a heritage property of this kind brings a particular challenge — the systems have to safeguard guests and the building fully, while being installed sensitively so they never detract from the character of the space.

GDC were asked to deliver effective fire and security protection that works quietly in the background of a historic setting.

Deliverables

What we delivered

GDC delivered a fire detection and security solution designed sympathetically around the building's historic fabric, with discreet device placement and cabling that keeps the protection effective but unobtrusive.

Everything was commissioned, certified and documented to recognised standards, and Tobar Mhuire is supported by GDC's ongoing maintenance so the system stays compliant and dependable.

  • Systems installed
  • Sympathetic fire detection
  • Security & CCTV
  • Commissioning & certification
  • Ongoing maintenance
